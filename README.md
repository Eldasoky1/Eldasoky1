## About me

I like backend work where raw data turns into something people can act on. On **CompliAI**, a B2B platform for EU AI Act compliance, I'm the backend lead and AI architect: I design how the React frontend, the PostgreSQL schema and the GPT-4o pipelines fit together.

I also did an **AI internship at FlyRank** (Jul to Aug 2026), working on the automation behind organic and AI-search visibility. I also build MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that let Claude work with real systems: a local machine, a LinkedIn account, a browser session.

I study computer science at E-JUST, where I got the basics: embedded systems, operating systems, databases, algorithms. I've taken those into real projects, including CompliAI (led a team of seven) and EcoLensAI (3rd place, Green Entrepreneurship Competition, team of four).

I work with AI coding tools every day. I set the direction and review what they produce.
